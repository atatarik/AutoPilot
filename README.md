Autonomous driving simulator. Each generation includes 100 cars with individual neural networks. After all cars of the generation have driven off the track, a new generation is created including the 10 best scoring cars of the last generation. After enough generations have elapsed, the cars should begin improving their driving abilities. 

Future improvements will include a penalty for all cars which sit around on the finish line, checkpoints along the track to encourage exploration and a time limit, since the neural networks are prone to stopping on the track and not moving at all.
